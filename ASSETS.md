### Prompt utilizado

Tool: built-in image_gen.imagegen (default mode)

Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: hero illustration for the Ganadazo startup landing page, a new complementary visual, not a logo.
Primary request: One premium chunky golden trophy with a purple geometric eight-point star/chakana emblem embossed on the front of its cup. Trophy stands on a broad violet circular pedestal. Exactly three golden coins float near the trophy and two tiny four-point light sparkles add polish.
Scene/backdrop: Solid uniform very dark charcoal-violet background #0F0E17. No environment, no scenery.
Style/medium: High-end modern gaming 3D product render, satin and softly glossy premium plastic and metallic materials, clean rounded geometry, expressive large handles, sophisticated studio light with subtle violet rim light.
Composition/framing: Square 1024x1024. Centered object group occupies 80 percent of the frame, fully contained with comfortable breathing room. Three-quarter slightly isometric view, compact confident silhouette.
Color palette: Main trophy and coins gold #FFB800, purple emblem and pedestal #6C2BD9, background #0F0E17.
Text: None.
Constraints: No letters, no words, no written branding, no logo typography, no UI, no characters, no money denominations. This is not a recreation or replacement of a brand logo. Show a simple abstract purple eight-point stepped geometric star on the cup. Premium smooth 3D quality with beautiful light and shadows; avoid pixel art.
